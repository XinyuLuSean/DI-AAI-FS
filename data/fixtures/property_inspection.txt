PROPERTY CONDITION ASSESSMENT REPORT

Report Number: PCA-2024-0312
Date of Inspection: February 28, 2024

Prepared by: David Kowalski, Licensed Home Inspector
License Number: IL-HI-2019-4428
Firm: Heartland Property Services, LLC

PURPOSE OF INSPECTION

This report documents the current condition of the residential property at the
above address following the water damage event of January 15, 2024.  The
inspection was conducted at the request of the property owner to assess the
extent of remaining damage and the adequacy of completed repairs.

WEATHER CONDITIONS

Clear skies, temperature 38F, no precipitation in prior 72 hours.

SCOPE

The inspection covered the following areas:
- Kitchen (primary damage zone)
- Living room (secondary damage zone)
- Hallway (minor damage zone)
- Basement (plumbing access point)
- Exterior wall section containing repaired pipe

GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

The property is a single-family residence, approximately 2,200 square feet,
built in 1987.  Construction is wood frame with brick veneer.  The home has a
full unfinished basement.  Overall structural condition appears sound.  The
property is currently occupied and furnished.

KITCHEN ASSESSMENT

The kitchen sustained the most significant water damage.  Repairs have been
completed by Springfield Plumbing & Restoration LLC (Invoice INV-2024-0283).

Flooring: Original vinyl flooring was removed and replaced with new luxury vinyl
plank (LVP).  Installation appears professional.  Seams are tight.  No moisture
detected under flooring with pin-type moisture meter (readings 4-6%, within
acceptable range).

Cabinets: Lower cabinets along the exterior wall were replaced.  New cabinets
match existing upper cabinets in style and finish.  Hardware is consistent.
Interior shelving is dry and shows no staining.

Countertops: Existing laminate countertops were retained.  Minor separation noted
at the joint between original countertop and new cabinet section (approximately
1/16 inch gap).  Cosmetic only — does not affect function.

Drywall: Lower 36 inches of drywall on the exterior wall was removed and
replaced.  Seams are taped and mudded.  Paint matches existing color.  No
visible moisture staining on new or retained drywall.

Appliances: Dishwasher and refrigerator were temporarily relocated during
repairs and reinstalled.  Both are functional.  No water damage to appliances
noted.

Plumbing: Repaired copper pipe section in exterior wall was inspected visually
through an access panel installed during repairs.  Solder joints appear clean.
No active leaks.  Pipe insulation has been added to the exterior wall section
(not present before the incident).  Water pressure test conducted: 55 PSI,
stable over 15 minutes.

LIVING ROOM ASSESSMENT

The living room shares a wall with the kitchen and sustained secondary water
damage from seepage through the shared wall cavity.

Baseboards: Original baseboards were removed and replaced along the shared wall
(approximately 14 linear feet).  New baseboards match existing trim profile.
Paint finish is consistent.

Drywall: No drywall replacement was needed in the living room.  Minor water
staining was present at the base of the shared wall and has been primed and
painted over.  Moisture readings in the affected area are 5-7% (acceptable).

Carpet: No carpet damage was noted in the living room.  The carpet extends to
within 4 inches of the shared wall and was not affected by the water event.

HALLWAY ASSESSMENT

The hallway adjacent to the kitchen sustained minor water damage to carpet and
lower drywall.

Carpet: A section of carpet approximately 4 feet x 6 feet was professionally
cleaned and dried.  No odor detected.  Carpet is in good condition with no
visible staining.

Drywall: No drywall replacement was performed in the hallway.  A small area of
water staining (approximately 8 inches x 12 inches) at the base of the wall was
primed and painted.  Moisture readings are within normal range (5%).

BASEMENT ASSESSMENT

The basement was accessed to inspect the plumbing repair from below.

Pipe repair: The copper pipe repair is visible from the basement ceiling.
Soldering work appears professional.  No signs of dripping or moisture at the
repair site.  New pipe insulation extends 6 feet in each direction from the
repair point.

Water heater: Functional, no issues related to the water event.

Floor drain: Clear, no standing water.

Foundation: No new cracking observed that could be attributed to the water event.

EXTERIOR WALL ASSESSMENT

The exterior wall section where the pipe burst was inspected from outside.

Brick veneer: No visible damage, cracking, or displacement.  Mortar joints are
intact.

Weep holes: Clear and functional at the base of the brick veneer.

Grading: Soil grading slopes away from the foundation at approximately 1 inch
per foot for the first 6 feet.  Adequate drainage.

CONCERNS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Minor countertop-to-cabinet gap in kitchen should be sealed with matching
   caulk to prevent moisture intrusion.  Estimated cost: $50 or less.

2. Consider adding a water leak detection sensor near the repaired pipe location
   and near the water heater as a preventive measure.

3. Annual inspection of the repaired pipe section is recommended for the first
   3 years to ensure long-term integrity of the repair.

4. The exterior wall cavity should be checked for adequate insulation during any
   future renovation work.  The pipe burst may have been partially caused by
   insufficient insulation in the wall cavity.

CONCLUSION

The repairs performed by Springfield Plumbing & Restoration LLC appear to be
complete, professional, and consistent with the scope of work described in their
invoice.  The property is in habitable condition.  No ongoing water damage or
moisture issues were detected at the time of this inspection.

The total estimated cost of recommended additional work is less than $200 and
is preventive in nature.
