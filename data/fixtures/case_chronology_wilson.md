# CASE CHRONOLOGY

**Case Number:** 2023-CV-04477
**Plaintiff:** Margaret Wilson
**Defendant:** Lakeside Property Management, LLC
**Date of Injury:** September 12, 2023
**Attorney:** Carlos Martinez, Esq. — Martinez & Associates

---

## SUMMARY OF CLAIM

Margaret Wilson, age 62, suffered a fall on the premises of Lakeside Apartments
due to a broken exterior staircase railing.  The plaintiff sustained a displaced
right hip fracture and underwent total hip replacement surgery.  This chronology
documents the medical treatment, property management correspondence, and legal
proceedings from the date of injury through the present.

---

## PRE-INCIDENT HISTORY

### August 2023

**August 4, 2023** — Lakeside Apartments resident Jennifer Blake submitted a
written maintenance request (Ticket #MR-2023-1104) regarding a loose railing on
the exterior staircase of Building C.  Request noted that the railing wobbled
when grasped and appeared to have corroded anchor bolts.

**August 10, 2023** — Lakeside maintenance supervisor Mark Torres acknowledged
receipt of maintenance request.  No work order was generated.  Torres noted in
internal email: "Not urgent — add to fall inspection checklist."

**August 28, 2023** — Building C tenant Richard Hayes submitted a second
complaint (Ticket #MR-2023-1189) about the same staircase railing.  Hayes
stated: "The railing is now visibly detached at the top anchor point.  Someone
is going to get hurt."

**August 30, 2023** — No maintenance action was taken.  Torres reassigned the
ticket to "Q4 exterior maintenance batch" with an estimated completion date of
November 2023.

---

## INCIDENT

### September 12, 2023

**September 12, 2023, approximately 2:15 PM** — Margaret Wilson, a visitor to
Lakeside Apartments (visiting her daughter in Unit C-204), descended the
exterior staircase of Building C.  Wilson grasped the railing for support.  The
railing detached from the wall at the upper anchor point, causing Wilson to lose
balance and fall approximately 8 feet to the concrete landing below.

**September 12, 2023, 2:22 PM** — 911 called by bystander Diane Farmer.
Springfield Fire Department Paramedics arrived at approximately 2:31 PM.  Wilson
was found conscious but in severe pain.  Right hip deformity noted.  Patient
immobilized and transported to Springfield General Hospital Emergency Department.

---

## EMERGENCY TREATMENT

### September 12, 2023

**September 12, 2023, 3:05 PM** — Wilson arrived at Springfield General Hospital
Emergency Department.  Attending physician: Dr. James Park, MD.

Initial assessment:
- Glasgow Coma Scale: 15 (alert and oriented)
- Vital signs: BP 158/94, HR 102, RR 20, SpO2 97%, Temp 98.4F
- Right hip: severe tenderness, shortened and externally rotated right lower extremity
- Neurovascular status: distal pulses intact, sensation intact
- No head injury, no loss of consciousness

**September 12, 2023, 3:45 PM** — X-ray of right hip and pelvis obtained.
Findings: displaced subcapital fracture of the right femoral neck (Garden
classification Grade III).

**September 12, 2023, 4:30 PM** — CT scan of pelvis obtained to evaluate for
additional pelvic fractures.  No additional fractures identified.  Moderate
osteoporosis noted.

**September 12, 2023, 5:00 PM** — Orthopedic surgery consultation.  Dr. Patricia
Reeves, MD recommended total hip arthroplasty (THA) due to displaced fracture
pattern, patient age, and pre-existing osteoporosis.  Non-operative management
was discussed but not recommended due to high risk of avascular necrosis and
non-union.

**September 12, 2023, 6:15 PM** — Patient admitted to orthopedic service.
Pre-operative workup initiated.  Blood type and screen, CBC, BMP, coagulation
studies, EKG, and chest X-ray ordered.

---

## SURGICAL TREATMENT

### September 13, 2023

**September 13, 2023, 7:30 AM** — Pre-operative clearance obtained.
Anesthesiology consultation by Dr. Lisa Huang.  Patient classified ASA III
(moderate systemic disease — hypertension, osteoporosis, mild obesity).

**September 13, 2023, 9:00 AM** — Surgery commenced.  Procedure: right total
hip arthroplasty via posterolateral approach.  Surgeon: Dr. Patricia Reeves, MD.
Assistant: Dr. Andrew Kim, MD.

**September 13, 2023, 11:15 AM** — Surgery completed.  Operative time: 2 hours
15 minutes.  Estimated blood loss: 350 mL.  Cementless acetabular and femoral
components placed.  Ceramic-on-polyethylene bearing.  Intra-operative X-ray
confirmed satisfactory component positioning.

**September 13, 2023, 12:30 PM** — Patient transferred to PACU in stable
condition.  Post-operative vital signs within normal limits.

---

## POST-OPERATIVE HOSPITAL COURSE

### September 13-18, 2023

**September 13, 2023** — Patient managed in PACU for 3 hours.  Pain controlled
with PCA morphine pump.  Transferred to orthopedic floor, Room 412-B.

**September 14, 2023** — Physical therapy evaluation.  Patient able to sit at
edge of bed with assistance.  Weight bearing as tolerated on right lower
extremity with anterior hip precautions.  DVT prophylaxis initiated with
enoxaparin 40mg SC daily.

**September 15, 2023** — Patient ambulated 50 feet with walker and moderate
assist of 1.  PCA discontinued, transitioned to oral oxycodone 5mg q4h PRN.
Hemoglobin 9.8 g/dL (down from 12.1 pre-op).  No transfusion required.

**September 16, 2023** — Patient ambulated 150 feet with walker and minimal
assist.  Able to perform bed transfers with standby assist.  Stair training not
yet attempted.

**September 17, 2023** — Physical therapy recommended short-term inpatient
rehabilitation due to patient living in second-floor apartment without elevator
(daughter's apartment at Lakeside) and limited home support.  Social work
consultation arranged.

**September 18, 2023** — Discharged to Springfield Rehabilitation Center.

Discharge medications:
- Oxycodone 5mg q4-6h PRN pain (30 tablets)
- Acetaminophen 1000mg q8h scheduled
- Enoxaparin 40mg SC daily x 14 days
- Omeprazole 20mg daily
- Lisinopril 20mg daily (home medication)
- Amlodipine 5mg daily (home medication)

## REHABILITATION

### September 18 - October 16, 2023

**September 18, 2023** — Admitted to Springfield Rehabilitation Center.
Attending physician: Dr. Karen Mitchell, MD (PM&R).  Physical therapy and
occupational therapy initiated daily.

**September 25, 2023** — Weekly progress note.  Patient ambulating 200 feet with
rolling walker and contact guard assist.  Ascending 4 stairs with railing and
minimal assist.  Hip precautions maintained.  Pain 4/10 average.

**October 2, 2023** — Weekly progress note.  Patient ambulating 500 feet with

reduced to 1-2 doses per day.

**October 9, 2023** — Weekly progress note.  Patient independent with all
transfers.  Ambulating with single-point cane for short distances.  Modified
independent with ADLs.  Discharge planning initiated — patient will reside with
daughter temporarily but at a different ground-floor apartment.

**October 16, 2023** — Discharged from rehabilitation facility.  Total
rehabilitation stay: 28 days.

Discharge functional status:
- Ambulation: independent with single-point cane, up to 1000 feet
- Stairs: independent with railing, one step at a time
- Transfers: independent
- ADLs: modified independent (requires long-handled shoehorn, raised toilet seat)
- Hip precautions: to be maintained for 6 more weeks

## POST-DISCHARGE FOLLOW-UP

### October - December 2023

**October 30, 2023** — 6-week post-operative visit with Dr. Patricia Reeves.
X-ray of right hip shows good component positioning, no loosening, no fracture.
Healing progressing well.  Hip precautions discontinued.  May progress to cane
as tolerated.  Referred for outpatient physical therapy 2x per week for 6 weeks.

**November 6, 2023** — Outpatient physical therapy began at Springfield
Rehabilitation Center with Dr. Karen Mitchell, PT, DPT.  Initial evaluation:
pain 3/10, hip flexion ROM 95 degrees, abduction 25 degrees, gait mildly
antalgic.

**November 27, 2023** — PT progress note.  Hip flexion ROM improved to 110
degrees.  Gait normalized with cane.  Patient began community ambulation
training.  Started low-impact exercise program (stationary bike, pool walking).

**December 18, 2023** — PT discharge.  12 sessions completed.  Patient
independent with home exercise program.  Ambulating without assistive device for
short distances.  Uses cane for longer walks and uneven surfaces.  Pain 1-2/10
average.  Released to independent gym program.

## FOLLOW-UP MEDICAL VISITS

### January - March 2024

**January 15, 2024** — 4-month post-operative visit with Dr. Reeves.  Patient
reports good function.  Occasional aching in right hip with weather changes and
prolonged activity.  X-ray stable.  No signs of loosening or wear.  Released to
annual follow-up.

**February 5, 2024** — Primary care visit with Dr. Maria Gonzalez.  Review of
systems: residual right hip stiffness with prolonged sitting, occasional use of
OTC acetaminophen.  Hypertension well-controlled.  Patient reports anxiety about
falls and avoidance of stairs when possible.  Referred to behavioral health for
fall-related anxiety assessment.

**March 11, 2024** — Behavioral health consultation with Dr. Alan Foster, PhD.
Patient describes persistent fear of falling, avoidance of stairs, reluctance to
visit unfamiliar buildings.  Reports disrupted sleep related to recurring dreams
of the fall.  Diagnosis: Adjustment disorder with anxiety, directly related to
September 12, 2023 fall.  Recommended: 8-12 sessions of cognitive behavioral
therapy.

## PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AND LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

### September - December 2023

**September 14, 2023** — Attorney Carlos Martinez retained by Margaret Wilson.
Letter of representation sent to Lakeside Property Management, LLC.

**September 20, 2023** — Springfield Code Enforcement conducted inspection of
Building C exterior staircase.  Inspector Thomas Grant documented:
- Railing anchor bolt failure at upper mounting plate
- Visible corrosion on remaining anchor bolts
- Non-compliant railing height (32 inches; code requires minimum 34 inches)
- Missing anti-slip treads on 3 of 12 steps

Citation issued to Lakeside Property Management for building code violations:
Citation #CE-2023-0944.

**October 5, 2023** — Lakeside Property Management completed emergency repairs
to Building C staircase.  New railing installed.  Anti-slip treads applied.

**November 15, 2023** — Demand letter sent to Lakeside Property Management, LLC
and their insurer, National Casualty Insurance Co.  Demand included medical
expenses, rehabilitation costs, lost wages, pain and suffering, and future
medical costs.

**December 8, 2023** — Response from National Casualty Insurance Co. denying
liability.  Insurer argued Wilson was comparatively negligent for not using the
interior staircase.
