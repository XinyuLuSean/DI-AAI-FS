## HOSPITAL COURSE

The patient was admitted to the neurology service for further evaluation and
pain management.  IV ketorolac and oral gabapentin were initiated for pain
control with good effect.  Pain improved from 9/10 to 5/10 within 24 hours.

MRI confirmed a moderate L4-L5 disc herniation with left L5 nerve root
compression.  EMG/NCS confirmed acute left L5 radiculopathy.  Neurosurgical
consultation was obtained from Dr. David Park, MD.  Given the patient's motor
deficit (ankle dorsiflexion 3+/5) and failure of 4 weeks of conservative
management, surgical options including microdiscectomy were discussed.

The patient elected to proceed with a trial of epidural steroid injection before
considering surgery.  Pain management consultation with Dr. James Rivera, MD
was obtained.  The patient was scheduled for a left L4-L5 transforaminal
epidural steroid injection to be performed as an outpatient on January 22, 2024.

Physical therapy evaluation was performed on January 9, 2024.  The patient was
provided with a lumbar stabilization exercise program and educated on proper body
mechanics.

By discharge on January 10, 2024, the patient's pain was controlled at 5/10
with oral medications.  Motor function remained unchanged.  The patient was able
to ambulate independently and perform ADLs with modified techniques.
