CASE SUMMARY REPORT

Case Number: 2024-INS-00142
Date of Loss: January 15, 2024
Claimant: Jane Doe
Policy Number: POL-889921

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION

On January 15, 2024, the claimant reported water damage to the first floor
of their residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield.
The damage was caused by a burst pipe in the kitchen area during a period
of extreme cold weather with temperatures reaching -15°F.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT

The following areas were affected:
1. Kitchen — extensive water damage to flooring and lower cabinets
2. Living room — water seepage through shared wall, damage to baseboards
3. Hallway — minor water damage to carpet and drywall

REPAIR ESTIMATES

- Kitchen restoration: $12,500
- Living room repairs: $4,200
- Hallway repairs: $1,800
- Emergency water extraction: $2,100
- Total estimated cost: $20,600

ADJUSTER NOTES

The damage is consistent with a frozen pipe burst event.  The insured
maintained adequate heating but the pipe was located in a poorly insulated
exterior wall section.  Coverage applies under the standard homeowner policy
for sudden and accidental water discharge.

Recommendation: Approve claim for covered amount less $1,000 deductible.
Net payment: $19,600.
